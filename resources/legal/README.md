# Rao (เรา) — legal & policy drafts

Status: **1.0-draft**, not yet published. Thai (`th/`) is the primary and governing version; English (`en/`) mirrors it.

> **Not legal advice.** These drafts were prepared by an AI assistant, not a lawyer. They are aligned with the Thai PDPA B.E. 2562 as best understood, but **have a Thai lawyer review them before launch** — especially the liability clause (Unfair Contract Terms Act B.E. 2540, Civil and Commercial Code s.373), the handling of volunteered sensitive data (PDPA s.26), cross-border transfers (s.28), and log retention under the Computer-Related Crime Act.

## Owner decisions / lawyer questions (no marker)

1. **Volunteered sensitive data (s.26).** The policy says we don't request it and only store it for display to the user's own space. Ask the lawyer whether an explicit-consent line is needed (e.g. a one-time notice: "If you write about health, faith or intimacy, you agree we store it only to show your space"). This is the main legal grey area.
2. **Scope of "no content of minors".** Terms §6 bans sexual/nude/exploitative content involving anyone under 18 (absolute). A broader ban on _any_ photo of children (e.g. a couple's own kids) was not adopted — decide if you want it.
3. **Push vendors abroad (s.28).** Push services and Nominatim are outside Thailand regardless of hosting; the s.28 paragraph covers them via "necessary for the service you asked for" — have the lawyer confirm.
4. **PDPC contact details** in `data-rights.md` were taken from pdpc.or.th (Nov 2026); re-check before launch.
5. **Paid features.** Terms §7 promises prices/payment terms before any charge; when payments arrive, add a payments section, the payment processor to privacy §4, and update ROPA.

## Owner decisions applied (2026-11-10) / การตัดสินใจของเจ้าของ

- Effective date + contact email: read from `.env` (`LEGAL_EFFECTIVE_DATE`, `LEGAL_CONTACT_EMAIL`) and filled in at render time (`{{effective_date}}`, `{{contact_email}}` in the Markdown). Fill them before launch.
- Minimum age: **20** (Thai age of majority).
- Hosting: servers in Thailand (provider name still open). Email: Google or Brevo (Sendinblue), both may process outside Thailand (s.28 wording kept).
- Backups 30 days, server logs 90 days, push log 90 days (pruned by `accounts:purge`), analytics 13 months: **recommended defaults**. Configure Plesk backup rotation and log rotation to match.
- Liability cap: greater of fees paid in the last 12 months or THB 1,000 (recommended default; lawyer to confirm).
- Session cookie: `rao-session`, pinned with `SESSION_COOKIE=rao-session` in `.env` (independent of `APP_NAME`). Name meaning: Rao = **Read · Answer · Open**.
- Encryption at rest: unknown, so NOT claimed in the public policy; still open in the ROPA.
- Sensitive topics (s.26): **explicit consent**, separate from the Terms: required checkbox at sign-up (`users.sensitive_consent_at`), existing users asked once before opening a space, withdraw in Settings → Privacy (spaces close until consent is given again).
- Minors: no sexual content involving minors (ever) **and no photos or videos of children at all**, including your own.
- Public pages hide any remaining `[PLACEHOLDER]` / `[OPTIONAL]` markers automatically; they stay visible in these files.
