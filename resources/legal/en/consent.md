# UI texts (for developers to copy)

## (a) Sign-up — usage analytics checkbox (optional, default: unchecked)

**Label:**
Help us improve Rao by sharing usage analytics (optional)

**Explanation:**
We record only actions, like opening the app or answering a question — never what your letters or moments say. Stored on our own server. Turn it off any time in Settings.

## (b) Settings — usage analytics toggle

**Label:**
Usage analytics

**Help text:**
Helps us learn which features bring couples closer. Never includes your content, never shared with third parties. Turning it off stops collection and deletes the analytics we've collected about you.

## (c) Sign-up — terms line

By creating an account, you agree to the [Terms of Use](/legal/terms) and acknowledge the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## (d) Account deletion confirmation

**Title:**
Delete your account?

**Text:**
Your account and everything you wrote or posted will be permanently deleted in 7 days. Changed your mind? Just sign in again within 7 days to cancel. Your partner keeps their own content. Consider downloading your data first.

**Buttons:** Delete account · Cancel

## (e) Export description

**Label:**
Download my data

**Description:**
Get a ZIP of everything you wrote or posted yourself — answers, moments, photos, comments, moods, dates, profile and settings — as JSON plus a readable HTML file. Other members' content isn't included.

## (f) Sign-up — sensitive personal topics (required, separate from the Terms, default: unchecked)

**Label:**
I agree that Rao may store very personal things I choose to write (such as health, intimacy or faith) only to show them to my own space.

**Explanation:**
Letters can be personal. We never analyse them or use them for anything else. You can withdraw any time in Settings → Privacy.
