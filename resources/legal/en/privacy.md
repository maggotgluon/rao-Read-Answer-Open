# Privacy Policy

Rao (เรา) is a private little place for you and the people you love. What you write here belongs to you, so we want to explain clearly and simply what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and how you stay in control.

This policy is written under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) (the "PDPA").

## The short version

- What you write or post in your space is visible only to the members of that space. Nobody else — including our admin — can see it.
- We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use third-party trackers.
- Usage analytics and location sharing are **off by default**. They are turned on only if you choose to, and you can turn them off at any time.
- You can download your data or delete your account yourself in Settings.

## 2. What we collect and why

### 2.1 Account data

**What:** your name, email, password (stored only as a hash, so we never see your actual password), passkeys and two-factor authentication secrets (if you turn them on), language (Thai/English), theme, timezone, birthday (optional) and whether to show your age, and your notification preferences.

**Why:** to create and run your account, let you sign in securely, and show the app in the right language and time for you.

**Lawful basis:** necessary to provide the service under the Terms of Use you agreed to (PDPA s.24(3)).

### 2.2 Your space content

**What:** daily answers, quest answers, moments (text and photos), comments, emoji reactions, dates and events (such as anniversaries and birthdays), custom prompts you and your partner write, your daily mood stamp, short replies under daily answers, and "nudges" you send your partner (at most one per day).

This content is visible only to the members of the same space. Your partner's answer is a "sealed letter" that opens only after you have answered that day's question. Most spaces have two members, but some spaces may have more (where this feature is enabled); every member of a space can see that space's content.

**Why:** to store and show your content to the members of your space — the heart of the service — and to keep your streak and shared calendar.

**Lawful basis:** necessary to provide the service under the Terms of Use (s.24(3)).

**Very personal topics (sensitive data, PDPA s.26):** we never ask you for sensitive data such as health, sex life, or religion or beliefs. But letters to the person you love may include these things. So before you can write in Rao, we ask for your **explicit consent**, separately from the Terms (a box you tick yourself at sign-up; people who joined earlier are asked once). We record when you gave it. We store that content only so it can be shown to the members of your own space, as you intended. We don't analyse it, categorise it, or use it for anything else, and you can delete it at any time.

You can withdraw this consent any time in **Settings → Privacy**. After that, your spaces are closed to you until you consent again, and you can download your data or delete your account. Withdrawing doesn't affect what was lawfully done before. **Lawful basis:** explicit consent (s.26(1)).

### 2.3 Photos

Photos you upload are stored on our server and are shown only after the system checks that the person asking is a member of that space. Your photos never have public links.

When you upload a photo, our server resizes it and saves it as a new JPEG file. Hidden metadata in the original file (EXIF) — such as GPS location, camera model or the time it was taken — is therefore **not kept**.

### 2.4 Location (optional)

This feature is off by default. Each member decides for themselves and can switch it off at any time. **Switching it off deletes your location data straight away.**

When it's on, the app rounds your coordinates to a grid of about 5 km and updates them at most every 30 minutes, only when you open the app. Your exact spot is never stored. We keep only your latest rounded location, never a history. To find your city name, we send the rounded coordinates to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service (a third party) , from our server, so Nominatim sees our server, not your device. We also keep your timezone.

Your partner or space members see only your **distance, city and local time**, and only when both of you share your location.

**Lawful basis:** your consent (s.19).

### 2.5 Feedback on daily questions

You can give a daily question a thumbs up or down. The admin sees only the total ratings for each question — not who rated it, and nothing linked to your answers.

**Why:** to make the questions better. **Lawful basis:** legitimate interest (s.24(5)).

### 2.6 Push notifications (optional)

If you allow notifications in your browser, we store your push subscription (endpoint and keys) so we can send you notifications — for example when your partner's letter arrives, when your partner nudges you, or when someone replies to your letter. You can switch off each type in your notification settings, or withdraw the permission in your browser at any time.

Notifications are delivered through your browser vendor's push service (such as Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, Apple Push Notification service or Mozilla autopush). Notifications are encrypted under the Web Push standard, so the push service can't read them. They only say that something happened and who it's from (for example "Partner's letter has arrived"), never what anyone wrote. We also keep a log of sent notifications (type and time) to check that delivery works.

**Lawful basis:** consent (s.19) for receiving notifications; legitimate interest (s.24(5)) for the delivery log.

### 2.7 Usage analytics (optional)

This is **off** unless you opt in at sign-up or in Settings, and you can withdraw at any time.

- First-party only: stored and analysed on our own server. No third-party trackers or ad networks.
- It **never includes the content** of your letters, answers or moments.
- What it records: actions such as signing up, creating or accepting an invite, opening the app (from a notification, from the home-screen app, or in the browser), answering today's question, opening your partner's letter, posting a moment, using a feature, and opening a notification; the days on which you opened the app; plus your device class (phone/desktop), whether the app is installed, and your browser family.

**Why:** to improve the app and understand which features really help couples feel closer.

**Lawful basis:** your consent (s.19). When you withdraw, we stop collecting **and delete the analytics events we collected about you**.

### 2.8 Short in-app survey (optional)

About 14 days after you sign up, the app may invite you to a short survey. It is shown to everyone and does not depend on your analytics setting. Answering is voluntary — you can skip it. Your answers are stored with your account and deleted together with it.

**Why:** to hear what you think and improve the app. **Lawful basis:** consent (s.19), which you give by choosing to answer.

### 2.9 Operational and security data

**What:** a session cookie for signing in, a CSRF cookie that protects against forged requests, your last-seen time, server logs (IP address and user agent), and error logs.

**Why:** to keep the service running, prevent unauthorised access, limit abusive requests, and fix problems. This data is needed for the service to work, so it can't be switched off.

**Lawful basis:** legitimate interest (s.24(5)) and, where the law requires it, legal obligation (s.24(6)).

### 2.11 What the admin can see

To run the service, help users and prevent abuse, the admin can see only:

- counts and usage trends;
- a user list: name, email, sign-up date, last seen, and number of spaces.

The admin tools **never show** anyone's answers, moments, photos, comments, moods or location, and we don't read your content. Direct database access is limited to maintenance, security work, or when the law requires it.

**Lawful basis:** legitimate interest (s.24(5)).

- We don't analyse or profile your space content, or use it to train artificial intelligence (AI).

## 5. Transfers outside Thailand

Some providers may store or process data outside Thailand — for example browser push services, Nominatim (based in Europe), and our email provider (Google or Brevo), whose servers may be outside Thailand. Our main servers, where your account and space content are stored, are in Thailand. In these cases we follow PDPA s.28: we transfer data to countries with adequate data protection standards, or where the transfer is needed to provide the service you asked for, or where appropriate safeguards are in place, such as agreements requiring the provider to keep the data secure. We send only the data that is needed.

## 7. How we protect your data

- Encrypted HTTPS connections.
- Passwords stored as hashes; you can turn on passkeys or two-factor authentication.
- Photos are shown only after a membership check; no public links.
- System access is limited to those who need it, and requests are rate-limited to prevent attacks.
- Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes, and two-factor secrets are encrypted.
- Photos are kept in private storage and are only served after checking that you're a member of that space; they never have public links.
- The admin can see numbers and the user list only, never letters, photos, moods or locations.

**If a personal data breach happens**, we will notify the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC) within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, unless the breach is unlikely to put people's rights and freedoms at risk. If there is a high risk to you, we will also tell you without undue delay, along with what we are doing about it.
