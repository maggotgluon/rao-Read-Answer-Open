# Changelog

Everything added or changed in Rao, newest first.

## [0.12.0] - 2026-11-11

- New home page for visitors: what Rao is, how it works in 5 steps, what's inside, privacy, installing on your phone, and common questions
- Fix: theme pictures could disappear when the site was opened from a different address (e.g. with or without www)

## [0.11.0] - 2026-11-11

- New theme: Doodle, a sketchbook with hand-drawn icons (Drawicons by weareheroes, CC BY 4.0)
- The theme picker shows the credit for themes that need one

## [0.10.0] - 2026-11-10

- Every page now ends with the app version (links here) and links to all legal documents
- First public release

## [0.9.0] - 2026-11-10

- Calendar stamps look stuck on by hand: each day has its own tilt and postmark angle
- Worn rubber-stamp ink on stamps, postmarks and the "Sealed" label
- Ink transition when you enter a space and when you open today's letter (off if you reduce motion)
- Paper, desk, buttons and the wax seal have real paper texture

## [0.8.0] - 2026-11-10

- Three new themes: Dusk, Daytime, Night
- New Valentine pictures for postcard reactions, dates and empty pages
- Mood stamps and survey faces redrawn
- Switched-off features are hidden instead of greyed out

## [0.7.0] - 2026-11-10

- Privacy policy, terms, cookies and data rights, in Thai and English (PDPA)
- Clear consent before writing about sensitive topics; you can withdraw any time

## [0.6.0] - 2026-11-10

- Download all my data as a ZIP, and delete my account (30 days to change your mind)
- Gently nudge your partner to write (once a day), and reply under today's letter
- Today's mood stamp
- A short survey after 14 days
- Choose whether to help improve Rao with usage data (never what you write)

## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-10

- New "sealed letters" look across the app: stamp logo, icons, and animation when you seal and open a letter

## [0.4.0] - 2026-11-09

- Long-distance mode: distance, city and local time of your partner (when you both share), plus 60 long-distance prompts
- Rate prompts privately; your partner doesn't see it

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-09

- Phone notifications: morning question, evening reminder, and when your partner writes or posts. The lock screen never shows private text
- Birthday on your profile; tie moments to your dates and see them again every year
- The prompt library grows to 300

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-09

- Three tabs in a space: Calendar, Today and Moments
- One question a day; you see each other's answer once you both write. Quests on weekends
- A streak that never shames: 2 free rest days a week

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-08

- Create a private space and invite your person with a link or code
- Thai and English
- Install it on your home screen
