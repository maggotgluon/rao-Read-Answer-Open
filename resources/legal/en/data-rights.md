# Your data rights

Your data is yours. This page explains how to manage it yourself in the app, and your rights under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA).

## Things you can do yourself in the app

### Export my data

Go to **Settings → Privacy → Download my data**. You'll get a ZIP file with everything **you wrote or posted yourself**: your answers, your moments and photos, comments, mood stamps, dates you created, your profile and settings — as JSON (to reuse elsewhere) and as an HTML file (easy to read).

It does not include content written by other members, because that is their data.

### Delete my account

Go to **Settings → Privacy → Delete my account**. After you confirm, your account enters a **7-day grace period**. If you change your mind, just sign in again within 7 days to cancel. During those 7 days you're signed out and get no notifications; what you already shared stays visible to your partner until the deletion happens.

After 7 days, we permanently delete your account and everything you wrote or posted: answers, moments, photos, comments, reactions, mood stamps, location, push subscriptions, analytics events and survey answers. Other members keep their own content, and the shared space becomes read-only for them. Copies in backups are overwritten within 30 days.

We recommend downloading your data before deleting your account.

### Withdraw analytics consent

Go to **Settings** and switch off **Usage analytics**. We stop collecting straight away **and delete the analytics events we collected about you**. Withdrawing doesn't affect how the app works for you.

### Turn off location sharing

Switch it off any time in Settings. Your location is deleted immediately.

### Turn off notifications

Switch off each type in your notification settings, or withdraw the permission in your browser settings.

### Correct or delete your content and profile

Edit your name, email, birthday and other settings in Settings, and delete content you posted yourself.

### Leave or freeze a space

Do this in that space's settings.

## Your rights under the PDPA

- **Right to be informed** about what we collect and why (s.23) — that's our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).
- **Right of access** and to get a copy of your data, and to ask how we got data you didn't consent to (s.30).
- **Right to data portability** — to receive or transfer your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format (s.31).
- **Right to object** to processing based on legitimate interest (s.32).
- **Right to erasure** — to have data deleted, destroyed or made anonymous (s.33).
- **Right to restriction** — to have use of your data paused (s.34).
- **Right to rectification** — to have data corrected, kept up to date and complete (s.35 and s.36).
- **Right to withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting processing done before you withdrew (s.19).
- **Right to complain** to the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC) (s.73).

## How to make a request

If you can't do it yourself in the app, or want to use another right, email {{contact_email}} from the address on your account and tell us what you need.

- We may ask you to confirm your identity, so no one else can request your data.
- We will act without undue delay and within 30 days of receiving your request.
- It's normally free of charge.
- In some cases the law lets us refuse or only partly fulfil a request — for example, if it would affect other people's rights, or if we must keep data by law. If so, we'll tell you why.

**About other members' data:** content written by other members is their data, so you can't export or delete it on their behalf. If something about you makes you uncomfortable, try talking to them first, or contact us.
